About Us

The Vietnam Youth Economics Summer Forum (VYESF) is an annual forum where students explore major economic issues through debate, collaboration, and policymaking.


VYESF 2026 takes a global perspective through a Model United Nations (MUN)-inspired format. Delegates represent different countries and work to understand competing national interests, negotiate solutions, and develop policies addressing economic challenges that transcend borders. Through interactive discussions and diplomacy, VYESF 2026 aims to give students a deeper understanding of how countries navigate an increasingly interconnected global economy.

VYESF 2025 – The Success of the First Forum

The inaugural VYESF brought together students for a day of economic discussion, debate, and collaboration, creating a platform for young people to engage with real-world economic issues.

2026 Theme

Governing Artificial Intelligence

AI is rapidly transforming the global economy, reshaping how businesses operate, how people work, and how governments approach economic policy. Its growth promises greater productivity, new industries, and opportunities for economic development, but also raises concerns about employment, inequality, competitiveness, and regulation.


VYESF 2026 explores how governments, businesses, and workers can adapt to an AI-driven economy, and whether developing countries can harness its potential without widening existing global inequalities. At VYESF 2026, delegates will explore these challenges through two interconnected economic perspectives:


AI and the Future of Employment

How should governments respond to labor displacement and changing workforce demands caused by artificial intelligence?

AI and Economic Development

How can AI drive economic growth while ensuring developing countries are not left behind?
